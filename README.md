# 🌍 Low Poly World — Ultimate Open World MMO RPG

A full-featured, zero-dependency 3D Low-Poly Open World MMO RPG built with [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) and Web Audio API. Runs directly in any modern web browser without build steps or installation.

---

## 🌟 20+ Key Features & Gameplay Mechanics

1. **Procedural 5-Biome World (500x500)**:
   - 🌿 **Sunlit Haven (Plains / Capital)**: Central hub with gentle hills, starter village, wild boars, and beginner shrines.
   - 🌲 **Whispering Canopy (Enchanted Forest)**: Mystical purple/teal moss, gloom spiders, and glowing ancient trees.
   - 🏔️ **Frostpeak Glaciers (Snow Mountains)**: High snowy peaks, ice crags, pine forests, and frost golems.
   - 🌋 **Molten Caldera (Volcanic Ashlands)**: Basalt crags, glowing lava pool, magma drakes, and the **Ancient Magma Titan** World Boss.
   - 🏜️ **Dune Sea & Oasis (Desert)**: Rolling golden sand dunes and mineral quarry boulders.

2. **Dynamic Day / Night Cycle & Starlit Skybox**:
   - Orbiting sun and moon with real-time dynamic lighting, sunrise/sunset fog blending, and twinkling night sky stars.

3. **Atmospheric Biome Weather System**:
   - Ambient particle effects that shift based on current biome (snow, embers, fireflies, dust).

4. **Action Combat & Monster Hunting**:
   - 6+ monster species (Slimes, Boars, Spiders, Frost Golems, Magma Drakes, Titan Boss) with AI state machines (wander, aggro, chase, attack, respawn).

5. **Hit Combo Counter & Combat Multipliers**:
   - Chaining consecutive attacks builds an animated Combo Meter (`🔥 5x COMBO!`) increasing critical strike chances and damage scaling.

6. **Stamina & Tactical Dodge Roll (`V`)**:
   - Execute invulnerability dodge rolls to evade monster strikes and boss ground slams.

7. **Mount & Riding Pet System (`R`)**:
   - Summon a mountable Low-Poly Direwolf with +60% movement speed and customized mount animations.

8. **RPG Progression & Leveling (Levels 1–50)**:
   - Gain EXP from monster kills, mining, chopping, fishing, and quests. Core stats: HP, MP, Stamina, ATK, MATK, DEF, Crit Rate.

9. **Interactive Skill Tree & 3 Class Branches (`K`)**:
   - **Path of Iron (Warrior)**: Cleave Slash, Whirlwind Spin, Earth Shatter.
   - **Path of Arcana (Mage)**: Fireball, Frost Nova (Freeze), Chain Lightning, Meteor Call.
   - **Path of Shadows (Rogue)**: Triple Piercing Arrow, Shadow Dash.

10. **Sandbox Building & Fortification System (`B`)**:
    - Project green hologram blueprints to construct Wood/Stone Bastion Walls, Platforms, Stair Ramps, **Heal Campfires** (rapid HP/MP regen aura), **Auto-Defense Turrets**, and **Mana Pylons**.

11. **Dismantle & Deconstruction Mode (`X`)**:
    - Easily dismantle placed structures to re-organize and reclaim resources.

12. **Inventory & Alchemy Potions (`I`)**:
    - Drink consumable Health Potions, Mana Flasks, and Elixirs of Swiftness.

13. **Quest & Bounty Board Log (`J`)**:
    - Real-time quest tracking for hunting, building, and defeating the World Boss with gold/EXP rewards.

14. **Ancient Shrines of Power**:
    - Discover Shrines across the biomes (Ignis, Zephyr, Glacius) to receive 30s elemental damage and speed blessings.

15. **Dungeon Ruin Teleportation Portal**:
    - Step into the mystical mountain portal at (100, -60) to warp directly to the Caldera Boss Chamber.

16. **Fishing Mini-Game (`G`)**:
    - Stand near lakes, rivers, or oasis waters and press `G` to catch rare fish and treasures.

17. **Interactive Character Sheet & Titles (`C`)**:
    - Track stats, monsters slain, structures built, and unlock prestigious titles.

18. **Social Emote System (`T` / Character Sheet)**:
    - Dance, wave, and cheer with animated character body poses.

19. **Simulated MMO Living World**:
    - Autonomous AI NPC Adventurers exploring biomes, fighting mobs, and chatting in the live World Chat feed.

20. **Mini-Radar & Fullscreen World Map (`M`)**:
    - Real-time radar showing player, monsters, shrines, and biome borders, plus interactive Big Map.

21. **Web Audio Sound Synthesizer Engine**:
    - Fully synthesized sound effects for swings, hits, magic, level-up fanfares, potions, gallops, rolls, and building.

22. **Settings & Customization Modal (`O` / `ESC`)**:
    - Adjust audio volume, mouse sensitivity, and day/night speed.

## 🚀 How to Play

Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) or host with any static web server. No dependencies or build commands required.
